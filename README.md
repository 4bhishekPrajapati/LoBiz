# LoBiz

Discover Local. Shop Better. Support Small Businesses.

LocalBiz is a platform that helps people discover popular local shops and explore their products online without roaming around the entire city.

From fashion and clothing to footwear, jewellery, and other unique products, customers can browse listings from nearby businesses, compare options, and book appointments to visit stores and check product quality in person before making a purchase.

Our mission is to connect customers with local businesses and make shopping more convenient while helping offline stores build an online presence.

# The Problem

Many popular local shops offer unique, high-quality products, but customers often don't know where to find them.

Finding good local shops can require travelling across the city.

Customers may not know which stores offer the products they want.

Offline businesses often have limited online visibility.

Customers may want to check product quality in person before purchasing.
